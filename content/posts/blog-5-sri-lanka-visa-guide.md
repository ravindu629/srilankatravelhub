## Do You Need a Visa to Visit Sri Lanka?

### The Short Answer: Yes, Almost Everyone Does

With very few exceptions, **all foreign nationals require a visa to enter Sri Lanka**, including tourists, business travelers, and those in transit. This applies regardless of the purpose or duration of your visit.

However, don't let this worry you. Sri Lanka has modernized its visa system to make the process as simple as possible for international visitors.

### The Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) System

Sri Lanka uses an **Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA)** system—a digital visa you can apply for and receive entirely online. This means:

✅ **No embassy visits required**—apply from home  
✅ **Fast processing**—approval within 24-72 hours  
✅ **Digital delivery**—visa sent directly to your email  
✅ **Simple application**—takes 10-15 minutes to complete  
✅ **Multiple entry options**—double entry within validity period

The ETA replaced the traditional visa-on-arrival system for most nationalities, offering a more efficient, secure, and convenient way to obtain travel authorization.

### Who Needs a Sri Lanka Visa?

**Need a visa:**
- Tourists from most countries worldwide
- Business travelers
- Transit passengers (special transit visa available)
- Visitors staying more than 48 hours

**Limited exceptions:**
- Maldives and Singapore nationals (visa-free for 30 days)
- Seychelles nationals (visa-free for 30 days)
- Diplomatic passport holders from certain countries

Always verify your specific nationality's requirements before traveling, as visa policies can change.

## Understanding the Sri Lanka ETA (Electronic Travel Authorization)

### What Exactly Is the ETA?

The **Sri Lanka ETA is an official electronic visa** issued by the Department of Immigration and Emigration of Sri Lanka. It's not just a simple approval—it's a legally binding travel authorization that's electronically linked to your passport number.

**Key characteristics:**
- **Digital format**—no physical stamp in passport
- **Pre-approved entry**—immigration clearance faster at airport
- **Validity starts on issue**—not from entry date
- **Multiple entries allowed**—can exit and re-enter within validity
- **Extendable**—can apply for extensions after arrival

## Sri Lanka Tourist Visa Requirements: What You Need

The beauty of the ETA system is its simplicity. Here's exactly what you need to apply.

### Essential Requirements

**1. Valid Passport**
- **Minimum validity:** 6 months from arrival date
- **Blank pages:** At least 2 pages recommended
- **Condition:** Must be in good condition (no damage, water damage, or torn pages)
- **Type:** Regular passport (not emergency or temporary travel documents)

**Pro tip:** Check your passport's expiration date right now. Many travelers forget this crucial detail until it's too late!

**2. Travel Details**
- **Return or onward ticket:** Proof you'll leave Sri Lanka
- **Accommodation address:** Hotel or residence in Sri Lanka
- **Contact information:** Phone and email

**3. Financial Proof (Usually Not Required for ETA)**
- While the online ETA application doesn't require financial documents, immigration officers may occasionally ask for:
  - Credit/debit cards
  - Cash (sufficient for stay)
  - Bank statements (rare)

**4. Valid Email Address**
- Your ETA approval will be sent here
- Make sure you can access this email while traveling
- Check spam/junk folders if approval doesn't arrive

**5. Payment Method**
- Credit or debit card for online payment
- Major cards accepted (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)

### Documents You DON'T Need for ETA Application

Unlike traditional visa applications, you **do not need** to upload or submit:

❌ Passport photos  
❌ Bank statements  
❌ Hotel bookings  
❌ Travel insurance  
❌ Flight tickets  
❌ Invitation letters (unless business visa)

Everything is entered as text information in the online form.

### Special Considerations

**For minors (under 18):**
- Require their own ETA
- Parent/guardian applies on their behalf
- Same fees apply
- Birth certificate recommended (carry during travel)

**For dual passport holders:**
- Apply with passport you'll use to enter Sri Lanka
- Cannot switch passports at immigration
- ETA is linked to specific passport number

## How to Apply for Sri Lanka Tourist Visa (ETA): Step-by-Step Guide

Ready to apply? Follow this detailed walkthrough for a smooth, successful application.

### Step 1: Visit the Official Website

**CRITICAL:** Use **ONLY** the official Sri Lanka ETA website:

**Official site:** eta.gov.lk

**Warning signs of fake sites:**
- Unusual URLs or misspellings
- Significantly higher fees ($100+)
- Requesting unnecessary documents
- Poor English or unprofessional design
- No official government branding

**Why this matters:** Third-party sites often charge 2-3x the official fee for the same service, or worse, they might be scams.

---

### Step 2: Select "Apply for Individual"

On the homepage:
1. Click **"Apply for Individual"**
2. Select **"Tourist ETA"**
3. Click **"Continue"**

**For groups:** If traveling with family/friends, you can apply for multiple people, but each needs individual payment.

---

### Step 3: Fill in Personal Information

**Section A: Personal Details**

Enter exactly as shown in passport:
- Full name (first, middle, last)
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Nationality
- Country of birth

**Important:** Use the name on your passport EXACTLY as written. Middle name variations or nicknames will cause problems at immigration.

**Section B: Passport Information**

- Passport number
- Passport issue date
- Passport expiry date
- Issuing country

**Double-check:** Passport numbers are easy to mistype. Verify twice before submitting.

**Section C: Contact Information**

- Current address (home country)
- Phone number (with country code)
- Email address (where ETA will be sent)

**Section D: Travel Information**

- Intended date of arrival
- Purpose of visit (Tourism)
- Duration of stay (days)
- Address in Sri Lanka (hotel name is sufficient)

**Section E: Emergency Contact**

- Name
- Relationship
- Phone number

---

### Step 4: Review and Confirm

**Before submitting:**

✅ Read all information carefully  
✅ Check passport number (most common error)  
✅ Verify dates are correct  
✅ Confirm email address (no typos!)  
✅ Review travel dates

**Common mistakes to avoid:**
- Transposed passport numbers
- Wrong passport expiry date
- Email typos
- Incorrect nationality selection
- Mixed up day/month/year formats

---

### Step 5: Make Payment

**Payment methods accepted:**
- Visa credit/debit card
- Mastercard credit/debit card
- American Express

**Fees (2026):**
- Most countries: **$50 USD**
- SAARC countries (India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, etc.): **$20 USD**
- Transit visa: **FREE**

**Payment tips:**
- Have your card ready
- Ensure card has international payments enabled
- Some banks flag this as suspicious—notify bank beforehand
- Payment confirmation is immediate

**What happens after payment:**
- Immediate payment confirmation screen
- Reference number provided
- Email confirmation of application received
- ETA approval sent separately (within 24-72 hours)

---

### Step 6: Receive Your ETA

**Processing time:** Typically 24-72 hours (most within 24 hours)

**What you'll receive:**
- Email with subject: "Sri Lanka ETA Approval"
- PDF attachment with ETA details
- Reference number and validity dates

**What to do with your ETA:**

✅ **Print 2 copies** (one for check-in, one for immigration)  
✅ **Save PDF on phone** (backup)  
✅ **Screenshot confirmation** (extra backup)  
✅ **Email to yourself** (multiple email accounts)

**At the airport:**
- Show printed ETA to airline check-in
- Present at Sri Lankan immigration
- Keep copy with you during stay

## Visa Validity & Duration of Stay

Understanding exactly how long you can stay is essential for trip planning.

### Initial Tourist Visa Validity

**From date of first entry:**
- **Duration:** 30 days
- **Entry type:** Double entry (can exit and return once)
- **Start date:** First day you enter Sri Lanka (not issue date!)

**Important distinction:**
- ETA approval date ≠ Validity start date
- Your 30 days begins when you first arrive in Sri Lanka
- You can apply for ETA months before travel

**Example:**
- Apply for ETA: December 1, 2025
- Receive approval: December 3, 2025
- Arrive in Sri Lanka: January 15, 2026
- **Your 30-day stay:** January 15 - February 14, 2026

### Double Entry Explained

Your tourist ETA allows **double entry**, meaning:

**Scenario 1: Single trip**
- Enter Sri Lanka once
- Stay up to 30 days
- Leave

**Scenario 2: Two trips (within validity)**
- Enter Sri Lanka (Start of 30 days)
- Leave for Maldives/India vacation
- Return to Sri Lanka (continues within same 30 days)
- Leave final time

**Key point:** The 30-day clock continues running even if you leave temporarily.

### Maximum Stay Limits

**Without extension:**
- 30 days from first entry

**With extensions (applied after arrival):**
- First extension: 30 → 90 days
- Second extension: 90 → 120 days
- Third extension: 120 → 180 days
- **Maximum total:** 180 days (6 months)

## Common Reasons for Visa Rejection (And How to Avoid Them)

While Sri Lanka's ETA approval rate is very high (95%+), rejections do happen. Here's why and how to prevent them.

### Top Rejection Reasons

**1. Passport Validity Issues**
- ❌ Less than 6 months validity remaining
- ❌ Damaged passport pages
- ❌ Passport expiring soon after travel dates

**Solution:** Renew your passport before applying if it expires within 8 months.

---

**2. Incorrect Passport Details**
- ❌ Mistyped passport number (most common!)
- ❌ Wrong expiry date
- ❌ Name spelling doesn't match passport

**Solution:** Triple-check every character. Have someone else verify.

---

**3. Previous Overstay in Sri Lanka**
- ❌ Stayed beyond visa expiry on previous visit
- ❌ Unpaid overstay fines
- ❌ Deportation history

**Solution:** Clear any previous violations before reapplying. Contact Sri Lankan embassy for guidance.

---

**4. Security Concerns**
- ❌ Criminal record in certain categories
- ❌ Previous immigration violations
- ❌ Travel to restricted areas
- ❌ Security flagged passport

**Solution:** Consult with Sri Lankan embassy before applying if you have concerns.

---

**5. Payment Issues**
- ❌ Payment failed or declined
- ❌ Incomplete payment processing
- ❌ Using someone else's card without proper authorization

**Solution:** Ensure card has sufficient funds, international payments enabled, and notify your bank of the transaction.

---

**6. Application Errors**
- ❌ Missing mandatory fields
- ❌ Inconsistent information
- ❌ Wrong visa type selected

**Solution:** Complete all fields carefully and review before submission.

### What to Do If Your Visa Is Rejected

**Don't panic.** Rejections are rare and usually fixable.

**Steps to take:**

1. **Read the rejection email carefully**
   - Check rejection reason
   - Note specific issues mentioned

2. **Correct the issues**
   - Fix passport details
   - Resolve any mentioned problems
   - Gather additional documentation if requested

3. **Reapply**
   - Submit new application
   - Pay new fee (non-refundable from first attempt)
   - Allow 24-72 hours for processing

4. **Contact Sri Lankan Embassy (if needed)**
   - For complex cases
   - Security concerns
   - Persistent rejections

**Success rate after correction:** Very high (90%+)

## What Happens at Sri Lanka Immigration (Airport Arrival)

Understanding the arrival process helps you breeze through immigration confidently.

### Documents to Have Ready

**Essential documents:**
1. **Passport** (original, valid)
2. **ETA approval email** (printed or on phone)
3. **Return or onward ticket** (booking confirmation)
4. **Accommodation details** (hotel name/address)

**Nice to have:**
- Travel insurance documents
- Emergency contact information
- Cash or credit cards (proof of funds, rarely checked)

### Immigration Process Step-by-Step

**Step 1: Arrival Card**
- Usually provided on flight
- Fill in personal and travel details
- Attach to passport

**Step 2: Immigration Queue**
- Look for signs: "Foreign Passport" or "Non-Residents"
- Multiple counters usually available
- Wait time: 10-45 minutes depending on arrivals

**Step 3: Immigration Officer Questions**

Common questions:
- "Purpose of your visit?" → *Tourism/Holiday*
- "How long are you staying?" → *[Number] days*
- "Where are you staying?" → *[Hotel name or city]*
- "Do you have a return ticket?" → *Yes [show booking]*
- "Is this your first visit?" → *Yes/No*

**Answer confidently and briefly. Be honest.**

**Step 4: Passport Stamp**
- Officer stamps entry in passport
- Stamp shows: Entry date, Visa type, Expiry date
- Keep this page accessible for visa extensions

**Step 5: Collect Baggage & Exit**
- Proceed to baggage claim
- Go through customs (green channel if nothing to declare)
- Welcome to Sri Lanka!

### Red Flags That May Cause Delays

**Avoid these behaviors:**
- Inconsistent answers to questions
- Inability to show return ticket
- No accommodation information
- Suspicious travel patterns
- Nervous or evasive behavior

**Most delays are resolved quickly with proper documentation.**

## Frequently Asked Questions About Sri Lanka Visa

### Can I get a Sri Lanka visa online?

**Yes, absolutely.** The ETA system is entirely online. Visit eta.gov.lk, complete the application form, pay online, and receive approval via email within 24-72 hours. No embassy visits or physical applications required.

### How long does Sri Lanka ETA approval take?

**Typically 24-72 hours**, with most applications approved within 24 hours. Apply at least 1 week before travel to allow for any potential delays or corrections. Rush or expedited processing is not available.

### Is the Sri Lanka visa difficult to get?

**No, it's very easy.** The approval rate exceeds 95% for tourists. The online application takes 10-15 minutes, requires basic information, and approval is fast. Common issues are usually simple errors (passport number mistakes) that are easily corrected.

### Do children need a Sri Lanka visa?

**Yes, all travelers including infants need their own ETA.** Apply separately for each child using their passport details. The fee is the same as adults ($50 for most countries, $20 for SAARC). Parents/guardians can apply on behalf of minors.

### Can I work on a Sri Lanka tourist visa?

**No, absolutely not.** Tourist visas strictly prohibit employment, paid or unpaid. Working on a tourist visa can result in:
- Immediate deportation
- Fines and penalties
- Ban from re-entry
- Legal prosecution

For work, you need a separate employment visa sponsored by a Sri Lankan company.

### What if I make a mistake on my visa application?

**If you notice before submission:** Correct it immediately. All fields are editable before final submission.

**If you notice after submission:** The fee is non-refundable, but you can:
1. Contact immigration via email explaining the error
2. For minor errors (one-letter typo), they may correct it
3. For major errors (wrong passport number), you'll need to reapply and pay again

**Prevention:** Triple-check everything before clicking submit.

### Can I extend my visa multiple times?

**Yes.** You can extend up to a total of 180 days (6 months) by applying for multiple 30-day extensions. Each extension costs $100 and can be applied for online or in person in Colombo. Apply at least one week before current visa expires.

### What happens if I overstay my Sri Lanka visa?

**Serious consequences:**
- **Fines:** $300-500 depending on overstay duration
- **Detention:** Possible detention at immigration facilities
- **Deportation:** At your expense
- **Ban:** Possible future entry ban
- **Legal record:** Recorded in Sri Lankan immigration system

**If you realize you'll overstay:** Apply for extension immediately, even if already expired (better than willful overstay).

### Do I need travel insurance for Sri Lanka visa?

**Not required for ETA approval**, but **strongly recommended** for your trip. Travel insurance covers:
- Medical emergencies
- Trip cancellations
- Lost luggage
- Emergency evacuation
- Accident coverage

Some airlines may require proof of insurance. Budget $30-80 for 2-week coverage.

### Can I enter Sri Lanka with a one-way ticket?

**Technically possible but risky.** Immigration officers may:
- Question your intentions
- Request proof of sufficient funds
- Ask about onward travel plans
- Potentially deny entry

**Recommendation:** Always have proof of onward travel (return ticket or ticket to another country).

### Is the ETA linked to my passport?

**Yes, electronically.** The ETA is tied to your specific passport number. If you:
- Renew passport after getting ETA → Must apply for new ETA with new passport
- Have dual passports → Apply with passport you'll use to enter
- Lose passport → ETA is invalid, get new passport and reapply

### Can I apply for someone else's visa?

**Yes, for family members or travel companions.** You can apply for others if you have:
- Their passport details
- Their personal information
- Their permission
- Your payment method

Many families apply for all members together. Each person still needs individual ETA and payment.
